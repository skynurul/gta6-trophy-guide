    Roadmap to platinum

    Play in this order. Story first, then the 100% checklist, then strangers and cleanup. Nothing except the one missable meditation is missable.

    Before you start

    • Save often, in different slots. There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, no mission replay and no New Game+. Keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotate the other 5 for free-roam cleanup.
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Do not turn cheats on while hunting trophies.
    • No online trophies. All 55 are offline and single-player. Difficulty is about 4 out of 10; the platinum takes roughly 200 hours.
    • Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage). Export a backup before you clear browser data — import brings it back.
